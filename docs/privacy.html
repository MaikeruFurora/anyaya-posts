Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 August 2026

Anyaya Designs builds live RSVP websites and printed invitation cards for weddings, debuts and milestone events in the Philippines. This policy explains what we do and do not collect, and how to reach us.

Our Facebook and Instagram app

We run a Meta app named AnyayaDesigns. It has exactly one job: to publish our own posts to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account.

  • It does not ask anyone to log in.
  • It does not read, collect or store information about the people who follow, like or comment on our posts.
  • It does not access anyone else's Page, profile or account.

The only credential it holds is our own Page access token, which lets it post to the Page we own. Nothing about you is gathered when you see one of our posts.

This website

These pages are static files hosted on GitHub Pages. We set no cookies and run no analytics or tracking scripts of our own. GitHub may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

RSVP websites we build for clients

When a couple hires us, we build an RSVP page for their event. Guests may be asked for their name, whether they are attending, how many seats they need, and sometimes a meal preference or a short message.

That information belongs to the couple, not to us. It is collected through the form service used on their page and goes to the couple's own account. We build the page; we do not run a guest database of our own, and we do not use guest information for marketing or sell it to anyone.

If you are a guest and want your response corrected or removed, the fastest route is to tell the couple who invited you. You may also write to us and we will help.

Deleting your data

We hold no personal data from users of our app. There is no account to close and nothing stored on our side to erase.

If you believe we hold information about you, email us at the address below with the words “data deletion” in the subject. We will look, tell you what we find, and delete anything we hold within 30 days.

To remove our app's access to your own Facebook account, open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites and remove it there.

Children

Our services are meant for adults planning events. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
